Who owns the robot?

The corporate questions in The Witch from Mercury

KOENJAZH

The official synopsis of Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury describes corporations building vast economic spheres in space. School life and corporate groups occupy the same world. This is an interpretation of a completed work’s public setting, not a new-series announcement.

When a school sits inside a corporate world

If companies provide the stage for education and careers, students can also be read as future labour. We see tension when the provider of opportunity sets the rules. This is not an allegation that every real industry-school partnership resembles the fictional relationship.

Technology and decision rights

The official synopsis names GUND-ARM Inc. It invites questions about whether creators, risk-bearers and decision-makers are the same people. We stay within published premises without using later twists or the ending as evidence.

The prologue’s point of departure

The official prologue places a machine’s trial operation beside a child’s birthday. It introduces personal time into a technology story. The Korean official site directly links the GUNDAM.INFO video below; regional and later availability can change.

Limits of the interpretation

This is not an episode-by-episode rewatch. It starts with official corporate, school and technology settings. No machine specifications or sales totals are invented. The separate original illustration does not reproduce an official series still.
