The bill for rebuilding comes before move-in: time, construction cost and rates in Seoul redevelopment

Checking the costs that run through a project, using records confirmed in Seoul

KOENJAZHES

In April Seoul said it would cut the average redevelopment period from 18.5 years to 12. On November 13 the Pungnap Geukdong apartments cleared a zoning designation plan five years after a D grade in their safety diagnosis. Over the same weeks the construction cost index hit a record and the base rate rose to 3.25%. This article checks time, construction cost, contractor competition, interest rates and the excess-profit levy against records confirmed in Seoul. It does not recommend a decision for any complex or person.

Time: an average of 18.5 years, a target of 12

On April 8 Seoul released a process manual for "Fast-Track Planning 2.0" and said it would cut the average redevelopment period, from zone designation to completion, from 18.5 years to within 12. It said the zone-designation stage had already shortened from five years to about two (Seoul Metropolitan Government). The Pungnap Geukdong apartments were completed in 1987 and received a D grade in a detailed safety diagnosis in December 2021. On November 13 a committee of the Seoul Urban Planning Commission approved, with amendments, the zone designation plan. The complex grows from 415 to 598 units (including 81 public housing units), with up to 40 floors and a floor area ratio of 340% or less (Yonhap, Money Today).

From diagnosis to zone designation took about five years. After designation come the promotion committee and association, contractor selection, project implementation approval, management and disposition approval, relocation and demolition, and completion with title transfer. Twelve years is Seoul's target, and the time for each complex may differ.

Construction cost: it rises after the contract too

The total construction cost of Banpo Jugong 1st Complex Block 3 (Raemian Trinyon) was 808.7 billion won (5.41 million won per 3.3 square meters) at the first contract in 2020. After three adjustments it reached 1.1746 trillion won, and in June 2025 it rose again to 1.2098 trillion won, about 50% above the first contract (Korea Economic Daily, Money Today). The construction cost index is also rising. According to the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology, the September 2026 index was 139.48 (2020 = 100, provisional), up 0.24% from the previous month and 5.94% from a year earlier, a record (Money Today). In general, when total construction cost rises, the part that general sale revenue cannot cover falls on association members.

Contractor competition: bid terms change

In all six Mokdong New Town complexes that closed contractor bids, only one construction company bid. For most of the complexes that picked a contractor, the cost matched the association's reserve price, and Complex 12 is about 1.7888 trillion won. By contrast, at Seongsu District 4 in Seongdong-gu, a redevelopment where Lotte E&C and Daewoo E&C competed, both bid more than 50 billion won below the association's reserve price (Chosun Biz, Newsworker, November 25). It shows competition can affect construction cost, relocation loans and the terms for paying members' contributions. Whether competitive bidding forms depends on a complex's profitability and how many bids cluster in the same period.

Rates: the monthly burden at 6% and 10%

The base rate rose to 3.25% on November 26, and the average bank mortgage rate in September was 4.88%, the highest since April 2012 (Bank of Korea). Rates were higher in the past. The average bank lending rate (excluding overdrafts) was 16.47% in June 1998 (Hankook Ilbo), and the average new mortgage rate was 7.58% in October 2008, the highest since the series began (GData, compiled from Bank of Korea statistics). In early October 2008 the top fixed-rate home loan rates at some banks passed 10% for the first time (Yonhap). Levies and people: the excess-profit levy

On November 9 Seocho-gu notified Banpo Hyundai, the first complex under the redevelopment excess-profit levy, of an expected levy of 17.9 billion won, about 223.75 million won per association member on average (YTN). The district takes objections for 50 days. According to Yonhap, no such levy has actually been imposed yet, and the National Assembly is discussing changes to the system, including whether to keep it. Whether the levy applies, and its amount, differs by complex. It means a cash item may arise separately from the members' contribution.

Scenarios: three paths

First, if rates stay high and construction costs keep rising, projects take longer and funding pressure grows. The grounds are the rate hike, the record cost index and bids without competition. Second, if rates fall and competitive bidding takes hold, terms improve. Seongsu District 4 is the evidence, and Seoul's 12-year goal is possible on this path. Third, outcomes split by complex. Even under the same fast-track planning, the time varies with the diagnosis grade, regulatory consultation and agreement inside the association. Which path prevails is not yet settled by the statistics.

Indicators to watch

Notices of zone designation, association establishment and contractor selection by Seoul and its districts show the time taken at each stage. The monthly construction cost index of the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology, the number of bidders and the winning price against the reserve price, the Bank of Korea's monthly mortgage rate, and the National Assembly's discussion of the levy are also worth watching. This article is not advice to invest or to transact.

Scope of verification

Figures in this article were checked against Seoul Metropolitan Government releases, the Bank of Korea, the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology and dated press reports. Some 1998 and 2008 statistics come from a compilation of Bank of Korea statistics (GData) and still need cross-checking against the originals. The repayment table is calculated by formula, not taken from published data. The scenarios give grounds and counterarguments together and are not settled forecasts.
