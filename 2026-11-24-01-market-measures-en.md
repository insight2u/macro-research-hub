# Market-stability measures do not guarantee a stock-market bottom

Financial Services Commission decision of November 23, 2026 · Economy

## Preparation, relaxed limits and surveillance are different tools

At a joint emergency meeting on November 22, authorities discussed increased stock-market volatility. Their response covers a market-stabilization fund, daily company share-buyback limits and surveillance of illegal short selling. Combining these into a single buying signal or guaranteed price floor would confuse the measures with an investor's hopes.[1]

The fund announcement concerns preparations to allow prompt activation when market conditions require it. It does not establish that the fund has already purchased shares. Actual deployment and timing need separate evidence.[1]

## Permission to buy is not an actual purchase

On November 23, the Financial Services Commission said its 2pm regular meeting approved relaxed daily buyback order limits from November 24 through February 23, 2027. Direct purchases may cover the full number of shares stated in the acquisition filing; trust-based purchases may be made within the trust's total assets. The notice also retains the statutory limit of distributable profits.[2]

A higher limit does not mean every listed company will buy. Company decisions, disclosures and available funds still differ. Permitted order capacity, actual purchases and price effects are separate questions. None guarantees returns on a particular stock.

## A surveillance announcement does not prove the cause of a fall

Authorities also announced three months of intensive surveillance of illegal short selling. They said they would monitor margin loans, unsettled purchases and forced selling, while strengthening advance warnings and investor protection.[1]

Closer surveillance is not evidence that illegal short selling caused the entire decline. The release discusses several uncertainties, including industry profitability, funding and policy risks. Individual wrongdoing requires investigation and evidence.

Newsfactory and Maebulshow also discussed market volatility on November 23. Their guests' forecasts remain forecasts; this article grounds its factual account in official notices. It does not declare a buying opportunity or promise recovery of personal losses.

## Personal risk remains

The measures address market functioning, contagion and misconduct. They do not remove an investor's borrowing, maturity dates or collateral requirements. Reviewing cash needs and contract conditions is different from adopting an optimistic interpretation of a policy headline.

This article examines the content and limits of market-stability measures. It does not analyze the final November 23 US regular-session close or returns after the announcements.
