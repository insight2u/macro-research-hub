HYUKOH’s 33 asks for 73 minutes

Today’s musician: a listening invitation, not a full-album verdict

KOENJAZH

HYUKOH released 33 on November 25. Yonhap and Rolling Stone Korea describe 14 tracks and about 73 minutes. This introduction uses release information and the public title track, not repeated listening to the full album.

Another numerical chapter

The album follows numerical titles including 20, 22, 23 and 24. Coverage describes the first new full-length album since 2020’s through love. The account of working across Seoul, Bath and Berlin and looking toward love after loss is the agency’s description. City names alone do not establish a musical style.

Follow the title track’s verbs

The available transcript of the official title-track video includes prayer, forgiveness and consolation. Yonhap similarly describes a cycle of anger, repentance and forgiveness observed at a distance. Rather than reproduce lyrics, listen for whether emotion ends in a declaration or a small change.

Another way to spend 73 minutes

Our suggestion is not to rank tracks immediately. Listening for transitions and changes of pace may restore the time that an album format asks for. This is an invitation, not a verified analysis of every track’s production.

Scope of the check

Track count, running time and release date were compared across two reports. We checked the official video link and supplied transcript, but did not attend a live show. Tour dates and ticket availability are outside this article.
