On a US market holiday, check the calendar before the price

Thanksgiving closure and November 27's early close in Korean time

KOENJAZHES

Anyone looking for a fresh US November 26 regular-session close on the Korean morning of November 27 should check the calendar first. NYSE and Nasdaq list November 26 as closed for Thanksgiving. The next session, Friday November 27, ends early at 1 PM Eastern Time for regular stock trading. No trading is not the same outcome as an unchanged index.

A screen date is not a trading date

If November 26 is a US holiday, the last close shown the following Korean morning may be from the preceding trading day. Checking a price today does not make today its reference date. Reports and records should preserve both the observation time and the session that produced the price. We do not fill this article with unverified prior index closes.

Convert the early close into Korean time

November 27 at 1 PM Eastern is Saturday November 28 at 3 AM in Korea. The 9:30 AM Eastern regular-session opening is November 27 at 11:30 PM Korean time. This is a stock regular-session schedule, not a claim that every derivative, bond or extended-hours service closes simultaneously. NYSE separately notes some options and later sessions on certain markets.

Broker services require separate checks

Exchange hours differ from the order, currency and extended-hours services available in an individual account. Nasdaq also warns that broker extended-hours schedules can vary. Check limit orders, validity and settlement through the relevant service. This article does not verify the capabilities of a particular account.

Do not overinterpret a shortened session

Holidays and abbreviated trading change the basis for comparing market data. The editors caution against comparing mismatched trading days or presenting a trading gap as fresh information. An official calendar alone does not predict price direction or volume. Checking the clock is data hygiene before it is an investment view.
