# Daily News Briefing · Five cabinet nominees named; Korean and U.S. stocks diverge

## 🏛️ Politics
- **President Lee Jae-myung and the Democratic Party:** On November 23, the presidential office named Kang Kum-sil as nominee for National Intelligence Service director, Lim Woong-soon for foreign minister, Hong Ihk-pyo for unification minister, Han Jun-ho for interior and safety minister, and Kang Yu-jung for culture, sports and tourism minister. These are nominations, not confirmed appointments. The Democratic Party said it would finish drafting an impeachment motion against Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae this week. This is the party's timetable, not a confirmed National Assembly voting date. [1][2]
- **Rebuilding Korea Party:** Reports said it proposed a cross-party joint group on November 23 to pursue Chief Justice Cho's impeachment. A rally at 17:30 on November 24 could not be found in today's public schedule and remains **unverified**; it is not reported as an established event. [3]
- **People Power Party:** It criticized the impeachment effort as an attempt to shield political figures. A confirmation hearing for justice minister nominee Lee Yong-cheol is scheduled for November 24. Party members of the Legislation and Judiciary Committee said they would ask about possible directions to withdraw prosecutions and his stance on impeaching Chief Justice Cho. These are the opposition's planned questions, not hearing results. [4]
- **Overseas:** Some political and security claims were excluded as **unverified** because primary and independent sources could not be sufficiently cross-checked.
