# Renewing a jeonse lease in 2027: check the deposit gap before house-price growth

November 24, 2026 | DailyShot Macro Research

For households approaching renewal of a jeonse lease, which involves a large refundable deposit, house-price growth is not the only important number. The difference between the deposit they will receive back and the deposit required for the next contract, and the cost of financing that gap, shape their housing choices. Tenants who signed at a lower price and have already renewed once should separately assess how far market deposits have moved from their existing deposit.

Deposits for early 2027 cannot be fixed in advance today. Nor can contracts signed at a particular time be assumed to have renewed on identical terms. What is needed is a distinction between actual contract records and assumptions, rather than statements that amplify anxiety.

## Five percent is not an automatic increase

The Housing Lease Protection Act allows the statutory right to request renewal to be exercised once and treats the resulting renewal term as two years. Rent and deposits under that renewal may be adjusted within Article 7. That article limits a demand for an increase to one-twentieth of the agreed amount.

This does not mean that every contract's deposit must rise by 5%. Whether there is an increase, and its amount, depends on the contract. A renewal using the statutory right must be distinguished from a new agreement made without exercising that right. The land and justice ministries' 2020 explanation treats consensual recontracting without exercising the right differently from renewal using it.

The fact that a household signed in 2023 and signed again in 2025 therefore does not establish that every such household will face the same legal situation in 2027. What matters is whether the right was actually exercised and what terms were agreed.

## Calculate additional deposits as scenarios

The funding burden can be expressed as:

**Additional deposit needed = deposit agreed for the next contract − existing deposit**

For example, if a household with a KRW 500 million deposit agrees to KRW 600 million next time, the gap is KRW 100 million. If it agrees to KRW 700 million, the gap is KRW 200 million. These are illustrative assumptions, not transaction prices for a particular area or forecasts for 2027.

The deposit gap is not necessarily the amount that must be borrowed. Available cash, repayment or extension conditions on existing loans and eligibility for a new loan also matter. Moving to monthly rent requires comparing the recurring rent as well as the deposit. Confirm the terms that actually apply with the financial institution and the other contracting party.

## A complex's name is not enough for a price comparison

The land ministry's transaction-price disclosure system says its records provide contract amounts, dates and floors, as well as reported lease terms, new-versus-renewal classifications and rent changes from the previous contract. Seoul's property-information service also includes fields such as exclusive-use floor area, contract type, use of the renewal right and the previous deposit in its period search.

A comparison does not end with finding two homes in the same complex. Match floor area, contract timing, floor and new-versus-renewal status. Asking prices also need to be distinguished from completed contracts. Public-data guidance says apartment rental records do not disclose building and unit numbers to protect privacy. Two public transactions cannot therefore be assumed to be successive contracts signed by a particular household.

Prices in 2027, which has not yet arrived, can only be presented as forecasts or assumptions. Treating a future price as a completed transaction can exaggerate or understate the renewal burden.

## Expiry does not itself mean automatic eviction

A contract expiry can increase housing insecurity, but does not itself determine eviction in every case. The Act provides for tacit renewal under specified conditions. It also provides that the lease relationship is treated as continuing until the tenant receives the deposit back, even after the lease term ends.

Whether an individual contract can renew and what termination means depend on notice timing, use of the renewal right, contract terms and the facts. A general market outlook must not be converted into a legal conclusion about an individual tenant.

## Policy should reduce the shock of moving

Looking only at price indexes can miss households' financing burdens. The gap needed at renewal, safety of deposit repayment, borrowing and monthly-rent burdens, and the costs of leaving an established living area should be considered together.

Policy discussions can start here: measure new and renewed leases separately, make deposit-repayment and dispute information easier to access, and broaden rental choices connected to jobs, education and transport. These are proposals to improve housing stability, not predictions that prices must surge or mass evictions must occur at a particular time.

Preparing for 2027 does not require one sensational number. It requires putting verified contract data, rights that actually apply and an affordable funding plan on the same sheet.

This is general legal-system and market explanation, not legal or loan advice for an individual contract. Source reference date: November 24, 2026.
