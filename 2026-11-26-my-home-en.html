Free rent: when does hospitality become a contract?

Today’s film: the published premise and trailer of My Home

KOENJAZH

Jung Yong-ki’s My Home opened on November 25, starring Jang Gyu-ri and Bae Jong-ok. This introduction uses KMDb and the public trailer. It is not a review after watching the feature.

Housing comes before horror

The published synopsis begins with delayed graduation and the loss of a dormitory place. A spacious Seoul apartment and a welcoming older host become a difficult offer to refuse. This is a fictional imbalance, not an actual rental opportunity.

The trailer’s gentle sentences

The launch trailer opens with questions about liking the house and invitations to feel at home. It leaves room to ask whether hospitality guarantees safety and how much choice a guest retains. We do not invent twists or identify a culprit beyond the trailer.

A home is also a space of control

Our proposed reading connects price with control: cheap accommodation alone does not make a relationship equal. This does not mean intergenerational living or free accommodation is generally dangerous. Fictional horror is not statistical evidence of a social trend.

Introduction is not a completed critique

Release date, director and cast were checked in KMDb. Runtime differs across sources and is omitted. Screenings and seats change in real time and are not promised. Judgments about the complete acting, editing and ending require watching the film.
