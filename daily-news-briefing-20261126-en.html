Economy: previous Korean close

The November 25 KOSPI closed at 4,697.51, down 308.52 points or 6.16%. The KOSDAQ closed at 650.64, down 15.24 points or 2.29%. These are the preceding Korean closes before November 26 trading, from Yonhap’s closing report.

Rates and US inflation

October’s new household-loan average was reported at 5.02%; the mortgage average was 4.98%. The Bank of Korea’s 4.64% average for all new lending covers a different group. US October PCE inflation was 3.8% year on year and 0.3% month on month; core was 3.3% and 0.3%. Yonhap and CNBC were compared. Expectations for a Fed move are not a final decision.

Trade: proposed action, not immediate implementation

USTR on November 25 proposed tariff actions for 14 economies for public comment. Korea’s proposal is up to 15%, net of MFN rates and existing Section 301 duties, with exceptions, not an extra blanket 15 points. Comments are due January 5, 2027; hearings are January 25.

US close: recovery from intraday lows

November 25 close: Dow 50,182.95 (+0.33%); S&P 500 7,182.78 (-0.25%); Nasdaq 23,585.80 (-0.62%), Yonhap at 06:12 KST. Nasdaq recovered most of a 4.6% intraday fall. Cboe’s daily file was only updated through November 24 (26.36), so no November 25 VIX close is asserted. NYSE is closed November 26 and closes early at 13:00 ET November 27.
