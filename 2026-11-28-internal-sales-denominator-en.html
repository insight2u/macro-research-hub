Intragroup sales are 35%. Leave out the denominator and the story changes

841 trillion won and 277 trillion won describe different transaction boundaries

KOENJAZHES

The same report contains both 841 trillion won and 277 trillion won in affiliate transactions. Neither is necessarily wrong. The Fair Trade Commission's 2026 analysis of business groups subject to disclosure examines 2025 transactions and separates dealings among domestic affiliates from dealings with overseas affiliates. Before debating scale, draw the boundary.

The domestic and overseas total

The analysis covers 2,871 affiliates of the 102 business groups designated in 2026, excluding companies without disclosures or sales and other exclusions. Transactions with domestic and overseas affiliates accounted for 35.0% of these domestic companies' sales, or 841 trillion won. Domestic affiliate transactions were 11.5%, or 277 trillion won; transactions with overseas affiliates were 23.5%, or 564 trillion won. Comparing a domestic-only figure with the total changes the conclusion.

Being subject to regulation is not a finding of guilt

Most domestic affiliate transactions by companies subject to the private-benefit restrictions used negotiated contracts rather than competitive tendering, prompting the commission to call for continued monitoring. This does not establish that every affiliate transaction or negotiated contract is unlawful. The commission recognizes efficiencies such as lower transaction costs while warning that the terms can enable improper benefits and harm competition.

Ask about the terms

Why did a company choose an affiliate over an external competitor? How were the price and terms set? How were the benefits divided among counterparties and shareholders? Rankings by share and by value also differ. This report is a map of where to investigate, not a list establishing unlawful conduct by particular companies.
