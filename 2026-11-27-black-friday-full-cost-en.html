Black Friday's price tag extends beyond the discount

Count customs, compatibility and returns before calling it a bargain

KOENJAZHES

The biggest number in a sale advertisement is the discount. But the real cost of a cross-border purchase does not end with the item price. The Korea Consumer Agency's checklist calls for checking import eligibility, local specifications, taxes, service and cancellation or refund terms before buying. This is consumer analysis of that checklist, not a recommendation of a particular deal.

First ask whether it works at home

Voltage, frequency, dimensions and standards can change local usability even under the same product name. The agency also recommends checking ingredients and prohibited imports. A working checkout does not guarantee customs clearance or domestic compatibility. Record the model, regional version and necessary accessories before comparing prices.

The total includes shipping and taxes

International delivery, forwarding, taxes and service charges may add to the item price. Rules depend on product, customs procedure and origin; one exemption figure cannot apply to every order. We neither calculate a particular order's tax nor assume all US purchases follow one rule. Comparisons require final checkout and import conditions.

Returns involve rights and transport costs

The agency distinguishes overseas sellers from Korea-based purchasing agents in legal treatment and cancellation conditions. Do not assume a change-of-mind return is automatic. Read the return address, shipping liability, opened-package restrictions, refund currency and timing. Forwarding-stage loss and damage compensation are separate terms.

An advertisement is not proof of trust

Very large discounts and demands for cash transfers are warning signs identified by the agency. An advertisement on social media does not guarantee a seller's reliability. Preserve order and payment records and packaging before and after opening to support a dispute. The editorial conclusion is not to hurry a purchase, but to widen the price tag being compared.
