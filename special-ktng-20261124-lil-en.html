From an eight-person experiment to a technology platform: how lil changed the way KT&G works

DailyShot Macro Research | KT&G Special 3 Published November 24, 2026 · Data rechecked: November 24, 2026 KST

A century-plus of making cigarettes is not the same as the ability to design an electronic device. When questions of hardware and software, heating control and user experience enter an organization that used to blend ingredients and manage brands, not only the product but the way of working must change. Seeing KT&G's lil as just a one-off hit product makes this organization's transition hard to see.

The story of starting in an eight-person product innovation office in 2016 and releasing the first lil in 2017 appears in both the 2023 interview with then NGP business division head Lim Wang-seop and the 2026 interview with NGP business division head Kim Dong-pil. But the timing and role of the two interviews differ. Only by distinguishing the person who explained the early platform's competitiveness from the person developing the next products on that accumulation can we read the organization's capability beyond one individual's hero tale.[1][2]

This article does not recommend buying or using lil. Nicotine's addictiveness and tobacco's health harm are issues separate from a product's technical progress. The object of analysis is how a latecomer company built a technology platform and accumulated a product-development organization, and the limits that remain when evaluating that achievement.

1. Why experience in brand competition was not enough

In a January 2023 Hankyung Business interview, then division head Lim Wang-seop explained that brand competition in traditional tobacco had shifted to platform competition based on technology. This is that executive's reading of the market, not an industry law that settles every tobacco company's strategy in one sentence. Still, it is an important starting point for understanding lil's development.[1]

If a brand manages customers' memory and choice, a technology platform handles together the foundation that makes the product work and the experience of repeated use. In an electronic device, the combination of heating method, battery, control and display, and dedicated consumables constitutes the product. That is why competing merely by changing the exterior and attaching a name is difficult. It is possible to analyze that an organization had to be built where manufacturing and electronics capabilities meet.

Hankyung Business reported that KT&G's tobacco R&D investment grew from 12.2 billion won in 2016 to 39.2 billion won in 2021. This figure must not be read directly as R&D spending on e-cigarettes alone. The same article described e-cigarette-related patent applications as 1,096 in 2021. R&D input and patents are traces of capability accumulation but are not indicators that automatically guarantee profitability and product competitiveness.[1]

Lim Wang-seop's strength can be found not in denying existing brand experience but in connecting it to technological change and executing. The interview introduces brand development experience including ESSE and RAISON. What matters is that experience in reading how consumers perceive products was combined with new technology-based products. The language of product development and the language of market research had to meet inside the same organization.[1]

2. What eight people made was not only a device but a way of collaborating

The product innovation office, the starting point of lil's development, was created under the marketing division in 2016. In the Hankyung Business interview, the initial eight-person organization is described as having grown to about 90 people including hardware and software engineers. This is the description as of the 2023 interview and cannot be reused as the current headcount in 2026.[1]

More important than the number is which job functions were added. To the process of making cigarettes were added roles for designing and controlling electronic devices, repeated testing, and verifying the combination of device and stick. The company's official 2023 job-introduction video also features an employee of the NGP business division's platform development center. The video introduction alone cannot reveal every internal development procedure, but it does confirm that platform development had settled in as the name of a distinct function.[7]

In an organization where different expertise meets, a design one department considers optimal may not be optimal for the whole product. Speeding up preheating versus maintaining uniformity during use, adding new features versus making them easy for consumers to understand, are problems to be solved simultaneously. This sentence does not mean we verified the company's internal performance evaluation; it is an analysis reading the structure of the product-development process.

So there is no need to consume the story that started with eight people only as the romance of a small team. The focus needed to ship a first product and the specialization needed to sustain a product line afterward are different. An individual's judgment must remain as repeatable procedures and technology for follow-up products to continue. That is why the unit for evaluating lil must go beyond a single launch event.

3. When and in which market did the latecomer's reversal happen?

Hankyung Business reported that after KT&G entered with lil solid in 2017, it overtook Philip Morris Korea to reach No. 1 in the domestic heated-tobacco market in Q1 2022. KT&G's share presented in the same article was 2.5% in 2017 and 48.5% in Q3 2022. These are figures showing a past transition, not the share of the whole market combining all tobacco products.[1]

To compare with the present, recent data must be set out separately. According to BusinessPost, which reported Q3 2026 results, the domestic e-cigarette stick market share was 49.1%. Without confirming whether the market name and counting scope in the 2022 article and the 2026 article are fully identical, we do not compute decimal-point differences as a precise long-term trend. Still, they can be used as data for checking the competitiveness of the domestic business at each point in time.[5]

NGP revenue in Q3 2026 was 294.0 billion won combined domestic and overseas. It splits into 173.9 billion won domestic and 120.1 billion won overseas. Stick sales volume was reported as 4.17 billion sticks combined, 1.94 billion domestic and 2.23 billion overseas. One must not assume the domestic-overseas composition of revenue and volume is the same, because the relationship can differ by product and by country-level pricing and revenue mix.[5]

These numbers show that a latecomer's product has settled as a business beyond the experimental stage. But they do not explain the profit of a single product, the cost that went into device discounts, or customer retention. Between the fact that revenue and share have risen and the judgment that the economics of acquiring one customer have improved, additional data is needed. To evaluate success properly, the boundary of confirmed achievement must be drawn first.

4. The 2026 task: user inconvenience over more features

In a September 2026 Edaily interview, division head Kim Dong-pil explained the difference between the product the team wanted to make early in development and the product consumers need. The interview is material conveying a company executive's explanation. It cannot be read as an independent performance test result, but it lets us examine how the problem awareness in the development process changed.[2]

The new product introduced at the time was "lil Tonino Lamborghini." ETNews' August 31 preview article and Maeil Business Newspaper's September 15 launch report conveyed the company's explanation of a method that uses microwaves to heat the inside of the stick and preheating of about 3 seconds. They also presented the condition that actual preheating time can vary with environment such as humidity. This article did not test the physical product, so it does not present that figure as if it were self-verified performance.[3][4]

The core of the Edaily interview lies in the uniformity problem behind the speed figure. According to the interview, the company repeated simulations and experiments to maintain a consistent experience during use while preheating quickly — an explanation that the task of preserving the experience from start to finish had to be solved together. For technological novelty to turn into value customers feel, conflicting design conditions must be adjusted.[2]

Design and the control display can be read from the same viewpoint. Reports that the new product has a metal exterior and a color display show the fact of external differentiation. But the fact that the name is luxurious or that it has a display does not by itself confirm user satisfaction or profitability. The value developers intended, the value consumers actually feel, and the profit the company recovers are separate stages.

5. The patent count is an entrance to explaining the technology, not a conclusion

The 2026 Edaily interview presents cumulative domestic and overseas patent applications in the NGP field for 2023~2025 as 5,302. There is also an explanation comparing it with 80 in 2017, but one year's figure and a three-year cumulative figure are not the same period. That is why we do not write that technological capability grew some multiple through simple multiplication.[2]

Patent applications differ from examination and registration, and the economic value of each right is not identical either. So an application count cannot be converted directly into a number of exclusive technologies or market defensibility. What matters is which technologies are protected in which countries, how they are used in actual products, and how rights relations with other companies are managed. With only the materials cited in this article, we do not judge the validity or infringement of individual patents.

The 2024 Invention Day commendation must also name its recipient precisely. Etoday reported that KT&G received the Minister of Trade, Industry and Energy's commendation and that then division head Lim Wang-seop attended on behalf of the company. Changing this into an invention award received by an individual alters the facts. It is right to read it within the scope of the organization's intellectual-property management achievement being recognized.[8]

When evaluating the assets a technology organization has accumulated, input, output and outcome must be distinguished. R&D spending is input, applications are part of output, and the profit and customer choices that persist in the market are another outcome. Declaring a transition to a technology company from a single number without confirming this linkage shortens the explanation while reducing accuracy.

6. Going abroad, product and distribution meet again

Lil's overseas expansion unfolded in the way that KT&G makes the technology and a partner widens the market. Official December 2021 materials described entry into 22 countries through collaboration with PMI. That is an achievement as of 2021 and cannot be written as the current number of countries in 2026. The September 2026 Edaily interview introduced it as entry into about 30 countries. The two materials are milestones at different points in time.[2][6]

The coupling of technology and distribution shows that competition and cooperation can exist simultaneously. Companies competing in the same industry do not lose the possibility of cooperation in every product and every region. However, since the partnership's contract terms and profit sharing are not fully public, one cannot conclude that lil's overseas expansion all produced the same margin.

There is recent change too. In the November 2026 announcement, KT&G said it would pursue overseas expansion of heated tobacco by adding independent local direct operations to the existing global partnership. This is not an announcement that the existing partnership has ended. It is the company's strategy of using both routes together. Which route is chosen in each country, and the actual results, must be checked going forward.[9]

Localization does not end at translating product descriptions. Country-by-country regulation, usage environments, sales channels and after-sales support differ. The ability to release new products quickly at home and the ability to operate abroad for a long time are connected but not identical. The technology platform's next test will be not the number of product launches but operational capability that is repeatable across multiple markets.

7. Is it an organization whose capability remains after Lim Wang-seop?

Evaluating the fact that Lim Wang-seop contributed to lil's early development and growth is different from attributing all subsequent achievements to one person. The fact that the leader in the 2023 interview and the leader in the 2026 interview differ rather lets us examine the persistence of organizational capability. If product innovation worked only on one individual's passion, the explanation would break off after a personnel change. If there are accumulated technology, people and ways of working, it can continue into the next product and the next executive's judgment.

The Hankyung Business interview shows the early judgment that chose a technology platform; the Edaily interview shows the current task of continuing iterative design while dealing with user inconvenience. Reading the two materials side by side, the elements of brand development, R&D, customer understanding and fast execution recur at lil's different points in time. This is an analysis reading the article's materials together; it does not mean we verified every decision-making process inside the organization.[1][2]

The next observation points can be organized into three. Does the new technology actually improve the usage experience, do development spending and intellectual property lead to sustainable profit, and do the overseas partnership and direct operations expand without colliding? All are hard to answer from launch-day reaction alone. Sales and costs after time passes, and customer retention and operating results, are needed.

Conclusion: the unit of transition is not a single new product

Lil's growth was a process of attaching new technology and new job functions inside a traditional manufacturer. The eight-person start shows focus; the accumulation of specialists and development capability shows repeatability. The past domestic market reversal and the recent NGP revenue are grounds for commercialization. At the same time, performance claims and in-house experiments, patent applications and the value of rights, revenue and profit must be distinguished.

Good corporate analysis shows concretely the problems an organization has solved rather than adjectives praising a product. In lil's case, those problems expanded from brand to platform, from first product to iterative development, from domestic competition to overseas operation. Only by including the point that technological progress does not erase tobacco's health harm can this transition be fully explained. The WHO explains that all forms of tobacco use are harmful and nicotine is addictive.[10]

Related video and the article's perspective

The official KT&G job-introduction video "KT&G의 개발자는 대체 뭘 개발하나요?" introduces the NGP business division's platform development center. We make clear its character as the company's recruiting and job-promotion material. This article connected the job and organization topics stated on the introduction page to the context of organizational transition, and cross-checked technical performance and market figures against separate articles and official announcements. It is not a summary of watching and transcribing the full video. We do not generalize the job and the person's role in the 2023 video to the entire current organization.[7]
