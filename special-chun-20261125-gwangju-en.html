Who Sought to Protect the Constitutional Order

The standard for understanding May 18 must place subsequent judicial judgments alongside the official labels announced by the state at the time. Supreme Court 96Do3376 examined the acts of the forces that seized power while addressing the suppression of the May 18 Democratization Movement and subversion of the constitutional order. Accounts that treat citizens who gathered to defend the Constitution as targets of subverting the constitutional order and the acts of authorities that suppressed citizens' resistance do not stand in the same place.

Martial Law Is Not Outside Judicial Review Either

The ruling explains that when the declaration or expansion of martial law is carried out to achieve the purpose of subverting the constitutional order, courts can review whether that act itself is a criminal offense. It means the doors of a trial cannot be closed merely by invoking political or military judgment. The standard that the purpose and execution of exercising public authority must be examined, rather than its form, leads us to re-read the resistance of citizens at the time.

Separating Overall Responsibility from Individual Orders

The fact that responsibility for insurrection was finalized is different from claiming that it has all been established who ordered collective shootings at specific locations and through what channels. This article does not include casualty totals or assertions of ultimate commanders that have not been cross-checked against separate official investigation texts. Leaving unverified parts open does not neutralize responsibility already established. It is a reporting principle separating what has been proven from what requires further investigation.

Accurate Language That Protects Memory

Expressions that revert citizens' resistance back into a riot clash with the context of the ruling. At the same time, there is no need to consume victims' suffering through sensational images or unverified numbers. Memory can be protected by disclosing rulings and investigative records and explaining layers of responsibility. This is the record-centered approach chosen by the editorial team.
