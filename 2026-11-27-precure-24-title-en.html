Precure's 24th series gets its title first

Only the title and slogan are public; characters and air date are not

KOENJAZHES

In a press release on November 26, Toei Animation announced that the 24th entry in the Precure series is titled "FUNtastic Precure!" (ファンたじっくプリキュア！ in Japanese). The catchphrase reads, "Shine! Everyone's smiles! Welcome to a fantastic party!" Details such as which Precure will appear are to be announced later.

What the announcement actually contains

The press release gives three things: the entry's place as the 24th in the series, the new title and the catchphrase. Anime News Network reported the same announcement as the 24th main entry in the franchise. The release points readers to the official Asahi Broadcasting site and Toei Animation's teaser site.

What has not been announced

The characters' names and designs, voice cast, story and broadcast start date are not in the release, which says only that details will be announced later. This article does not fill the gaps with patterns from earlier series and does not guess a date.

How to read a title and a slogan

Words like "smiles" and "party" are the first impression the producer chose. They are not evidence of the story's direction or quality. The editors do not judge a work by its title and quote the slogan as the official wording.

What fans can check next

Watch for follow-up notices on the official sites. When characters and a schedule appear, check the schedule and how to watch then. This article is not a recommendation to buy or watch anything, and no official logo or image has been reproduced.
