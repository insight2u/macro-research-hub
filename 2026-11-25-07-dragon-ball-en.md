## A 42-volume adventure that began in manga

Dragon Ball is a manga drawn by Akira Toriyama, serialized in Shueisha's 'Weekly Shonen Jump' from 1984 to 1995, collected in 42 volumes. The TV anime, produced by Toei Animation, aired from February 24, 1986 to April 19, 1989, for a total of 153 episodes.

Toei's official synopsis begins like this. Deep in the mountains lives a boy who grew up wild and completely innocent: Son Goku. One day a girl searching for the Dragon Balls, Bulma, arrives in a strange car. Gather all seven balls scattered around the world and chant the magic words, and a sacred dragon appears to grant one wish. Goku's ball was one of the seven, but it was a memento of the grandfather who raised him, so he could not give it up. In the end the two set out on a journey to find the Dragon Balls together. That was the beginning of everything.

## The creator has gone, but Shenron still hears wishes

Akira Toriyama passed away on March 1, 2024, at the age of 68; Shueisha, Reuters and others reported the news on March 8 that year. Yet the world he drew keeps expanding, and the thrill of gathering seven balls still lives in the hearts of grown men. Before Dragon Ball, a middle-aged man becomes a boy again today.
