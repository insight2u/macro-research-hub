Daltanious returns, but preorder and release are different dates

A collector's calendar separates announcement, order and delivery

KOENJAZHES

Bandai Spirits' Tamashii Web lists Chogokin Future Robot Daltanious for preorders starting December 1 and a planned Japanese release in June 2027. It does not mean the product is already released today, November 27. Nostalgic announcements need a clear separation between excitement and actual availability.

What the manufacturer means by a revival

The official account describes a return of Popy's standard Chogokin Daltanious released in 1979. It lists a height of approximately 150 mm, die-cast and ABS construction, and weapons not supplied with the earlier toy. These are manufacturer specifications and intentions. This article is not a hands-on test of weight or paint quality.

The same robot name does not promise the same mechanism

The page describes firing fists and missiles, weapons and opening wing parts. Not every toy implements everything in the fictional robot's setting. If a particular combination or transformation matters, check that the exact product description includes it. We do not add an undocumented complete-combination feature.

Japanese information is not Korean stock

The maker says the information concerns Japan and regional availability must be checked separately. A Japanese preorder opening does not guarantee a Korean shop will take orders that day. We have not verified Korean prices, shipping, import charges or inventory. A planned month is not a confirmed delivery date.

Checking does not remove the pleasure of collecting

An announcement is when news arrives, a preorder is when terms can be considered, and delivery is when the item is received. Keeping those dates separate prevents waiting time from being mistaken for evidence of quality or rarity. The editors do not urge a purchase or predict resale profits. We link the official product page without newly reproducing copyrighted product photographs.
