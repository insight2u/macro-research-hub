The next robot contest is control, not tricks

What Samsung’s personnel release says about hardware and whole-body control

KOENJAZH

Samsung’s November 24 announcement names robotics hardware and AI/control among its future skills. A short walking demonstration and dependable factory work are different tests.

Two roles in the release

The company describes actuator design and control on the hardware side, and humanoid walking, whole-body control and reinforcement learning on the AI side. These are the company’s reasons for promotions, not independent evidence of a product launch, shipments or profit.

Walking is not a factory uptime measure

Our industrial question is repeatability: sensing, control and actuators must work together. A successful demonstration does not establish production uptime. Task limits, recovery after failure and the conditions for working near people need separate answers.

Look at the system

Appointing hardware and control specialists suggests why one strong component is insufficient. Personnel priorities still differ from paid customer orders and investment execution. Investors should examine delivery conditions and maintenance responsibility rather than the robot label alone.

Historical comparison only

The attached YonhapnewsTV video covers Samsung promotions in 2017, when semiconductor results also drove rewards. It provides a comparison of corporate language, not proof of current robotics revenue. This is a reading of public material, not a factory visit.
