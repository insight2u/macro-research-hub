28 November morning briefing: markets rebound, household costs remain

Four political strands, market closes, weekend plans and weather | Checked 06:15 KST

KOENJAZHES

Korean and US shares rebounded, but household disposable income did not rise with them. Politics remains focused on reform implementation, party discipline and a proposed impeachment timetable. On Saturday morning, distinguish yesterday's closes from today's plans, and decisions from demands.

Lee Jae-myung and the Democratic Party: instructions are not implementation

The presidential office's official 27 November briefing says President Lee ordered a review of extending appropriate-wage rules for government projects to construction commissioned by all ministries. He also directed that the Social Reform Committee become a presidential committee and receive more staff. These instructions do not mean that higher wages or institutional changes have already taken effect.

The Democratic Party's ethics tribunal voted for a two-year suspension of supreme council member Choi Min-hee's party membership over remarks it said damaged the party's dignity, Yonhap reported. Choi rejected the decision and said she would seek reconsideration. The initial decision and the final outcome after review are different stages. Separately, Yonhap reported late on 27 November that Lee said ministries should in principle accept the Capital Market Transformation Committee's reform proposals, with disagreements reported directly to the presidential office.

Rebuilding Korea Party: a demand is not an agreed vote

Party leader Shin Jang-sik reiterated on 27 November that an impeachment motion against Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae should be handled on 3 December. Newspim reported continuing working-level contacts with the Democratic Party over a joint initiative, but differences over timing. December 3 is therefore not presented as a parliamentary vote date already agreed.

Cho Kuk, head of the party's policy institute, said in a radio interview that the president should meet Kim Min-seok and Chung Chung-rae to address Democratic Party infighting. This was a request for mediation, not an announcement of a scheduled presidential meeting. Political judgments belong to the speaker; agreements, motions and votes each need their own confirmation.

People Power Party: conflict and unproven allegations

Yonhap reported continuing conflict between party factions on 27 November, with attention on a planned lawmakers' meeting on 2 December. Claims of intervention in disciplinary proceedings face denials from the leadership. Reporting the existence of these claims does not establish that either side's allegations are true.

The published 28 November schedule includes a Daejeon policy briefing, a meeting with merchants and a 3 pm rally. Assertions of unconstitutionality in a rally's title are the organizers' political claims, not a court judgment.

International politics and trade: opposition is not a policy reversal

The Korea Center for International Finance's 27 November report described Chinese and EU opposition to US overcapacity tariffs. Trade disputes can affect prices and investment uncertainty, but a statement of opposition is neither tariff withdrawal nor a completed agreement. The report's market table covers the Thanksgiving holiday period and must not be mixed with the 27 November New York close below.

KIEP's 2027 outlook describes AI investment and trade as growth supports, while energy and financing costs can weigh on spending and investment. A forecast is conditional. A data-center investment announcement cannot immediately be counted as profits or household income.

Korean markets and households: different numbers, different questions

On 27 November, the KOSPI closed at 4,915.68, up 204.47 points or 4.34%; the KOSDAQ closed at 708.99, up 53.28 points or 8.13%. Yonhap reported a KOSDAQ buy-side sidecar and net foreign and institutional purchases. One strong day does not establish that longer-term risks have disappeared.

The Seoul USD/KRW 3:30 pm reference rate was 1,337.9, down 4.8 won. Do not mix this with rates from a different closing time. These are Friday observations, not live Saturday quotes.

The third-quarter household survey showed average monthly income of KRW 5.48 million, up 0.8%, but disposable income of KRW 4.306 million, down 1.7%. Consumption spending was KRW 3.03 million and the average propensity to consume was 70.4%. A stock-market rebound and household purchasing power are not the same measure.

US, Asia and Europe: separate regions and trading clocks

CNBC reported 27 November closes of 50,774.94 for the Dow (+1.18%), 7,297.49 for the S&P 500 (+1.60%) and 24,129.50 for the Nasdaq Composite (+2.31%). The shortened post-Thanksgiving session ended at 1 pm US Eastern time, or 3 am KST on 28 November. Consider thin holiday trading alongside sharp AI-stock swings.

Etoday reported the Nikkei225 at58,711.11, up2.11%, on27November. The rates and regional supplements below identify timestamps and distinguish official material from reported quotes. Stock, bond, oil and gold prices are not a single synchronized snapshot.

Rates, volatility and energy: one day, different conventions

Korean final quoted government-bond yields on 27 November were 3.685% for three years (+6.3 bp) and 3.832% for ten years (+4.2 bp). The Bank of Korea raised its policy rate from 3.00% to 3.25% on 26 November. The Fed’s official 28 October target range was 3.75–4.00%. Expectations for the next meeting are not the current policy rate.

YTN’s 04:19 KST report on 28 November put the US ten-year yield near the equity close at 4.93%, the VIX at 23.81 (-11.16%) and the SOX up 4.69%. Investing’s closed quote also showed VIX 23.81. Since Cboe’s daily file was not yet updated, this is labeled a reported quote, not a verified official daily-file close.

CNBC reported 27 November crude futures settlements of $95.44 for Brent (-1.2%) and $83.08 for WTI (about -1% from Wednesday). WTI had no Thursday settlement. Do not confuse intraday Brent above $97 with settlement. Gold material contained conflicting date descriptions, so no supposedly final current price is filled in.

Regional supplement: rebounds were not uniform

China, Japan and Taiwan: Etoday reported 27 November closes of 3,606.49 for the Shanghai Composite (+3.73%), 58,711.11 for the Nikkei (+2.11%) and 39,308.08 for Taiwan’s TAIEX (+0.40%). Its Hong Kong quote was intraday and is not presented as a close.

Europe: Reuters reported 27 November closes of 8,156.09 for the CAC 40 (+0.58%), with the DAX up 0.86% and Stoxx 600 up 1.07%. US quotes at the European close were still intraday, unlike the final New York values above.

Southeast Asia and India: CNBC Indonesia reported Jakarta’s composite at 5,338.46, up 1.84%, on 27 November. Reuters reporting put India’s Nifty 50 at 25,607.2 (-1.07%) and Sensex at 83,339.26 (-1.17%). Asia did not rise uniformly.

Russia and Latin America: MOEX 2,331.76 (-1.06%) in Fisco’s 27 November morning report was Russia’s 26 November close. Reuters’ 27 November Brazil report described an intraday reaction to Trabuco’s nomination as the next finance minister, not a final close. Where comparable dated evidence is unavailable, show the timing difference or omit the number.

Society and everyday finance: three locks protect different doors

The Financial Services Commission's credit-transaction, remote-account-opening and open-banking blocking services cover different transactions. Enabling one does not prevent every type of fraud. Start from an official app you already use or a bank number verified independently, not a link supplied by a caller.

Fair Trade Commission business-group data distinguish KRW 841 trillion in total domestic and overseas affiliate transactions from KRW 277 trillion between domestic affiliates. Neither can be relabeled as the scale of wrongdoing. The proposed KS standard for robot-training data is also at the consultation stage, not already an operative rule.

Saturday plans and culture: check before leaving

News1 and Newsis schedules list a Democratic Party youth-member town hall at 2 pm, People Power Party events in Daejeon, and no public events for the Rebuilding Korea Party's leader and floor leader. No public event does not mean no work. Organizers may change schedules.

The National Gugak Center's official calendar lists Saturday Masterpieces at 3 pm in Umyeondang and an English museum tour at 2 pm. Check official booking and operating information for seats and conditions. The Seoul Baekje Museum's exhibition on the Samguk Sagi and Samguk Yusa continues. Our book feature on Algorithmic Disobedience uses public bibliographic material, contents and an author interview; it is a reading guide, not a claim to have reviewed the whole book.

Weather: Seoul is not the rain-and-snow map

The Korea Meteorological Administration's 5 am forecast for Sindaebang 2-dong, Dongjak-gu, Seoul, gives clear weather and a high of 9°C today. A neighborhood forecast is not a live observed temperature for all Seoul. The national forecast warns of rain or snow mainly in Chungcheong, Jeolla, inland Gyeongsang and Jeju, plus strong winds and rough seas. Check timing along your route and watch for icy roads. Most of the country is forecast to fall below freezing tomorrow morning.
