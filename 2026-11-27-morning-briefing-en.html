November 27 morning briefing: the calendar behind the rate rise

Politics, markets, trade, daily life, culture and Seoul weather | checked at 07:50 KST

KOENJAZHES

After Korea's rate rise comes the cost households and companies must bear. America's new tariff proposal is still subject to consultation, while school meals and supermarket visits need extra planning today. The morning's news calls for a clear distinction between announcements and decisions, forecasts and results.

Politics: how public investment shares its returns

The presidential office's account of the November 26 meeting called for faster work on seven SEED projects, tailored plans and public-private cooperation. Officials discussed equity investment and rules for sharing the returns from state support. An instruction to move faster is not evidence that investment returns or jobs have already materialized.

Yonhap reported approval of parliamentary hearing requests for five nominees: Kang Kum-sil for intelligence chief, Lim Woong-soon for foreign affairs, Hong Ihk-pyo for unification, Han Jun-ho for interior affairs and Kang Yu-jung for culture. The start of hearings is not a completed appointment. Unverified misconduct or resignation claims are excluded. Deferred review of future-response fund projects likewise means neither final approval nor abolition.

Markets: read closing prices beside borrowing costs

At Korea's November 26 close, the KOSPI stood at 4,711.21, up 0.29%, and KOSDAQ at 655.71, up 0.78%. The won-dollar reference rate at 15:30 was 1,342.7 won per dollar, down 0.5 won. These are previous-day observations, not live November 27 prices.

The Bank of Korea raised its policy rate from 3.00% to 3.25%. Its growth forecasts are 3.9% for 2026 and 4.1% for 2027. The rate is a decision; growth remains a forecast. Three-year government bonds closed at 3.622% and ten-year bonds at 3.790%, a gap of 16.8 basis points. Actual deposit and loan rates also depend on product repricing dates and margins.

US exchanges were closed on November 26 for Thanksgiving. The November 27 equity session ends early at 13:00 Eastern, or 03:00 on November 28 in Korea. BEA's second estimate puts third-quarter real GDP growth at a 4.3% annualized quarter-on-quarter rate. It is not a year-on-year rate or the unannualized quarterly increase.

Trade: a proposal is not a final tariff bill

USTR released findings and proposed action concerning 14 economies on November 25. The Korea proposal is up to 15%, accounting for existing duties and exemptions. It does not mean a fresh 15% is added to every product. Written comments are due January 5, 2027; hearings begin January 25. A hearing date is not a final implementation date.

Germany's December GfK consumer confidence reading was reported at -30.2, improving from a revised -30.9. Improvement and a still-low level belong in the same picture. Tariff proposals also differ across China, Japan, Southeast Asia and India. Their rates, exemptions and negotiating conditions cannot be collapsed into one number.

Daily life: check school notices and store operations

Homeplus said cash shortages for purchasing goods would lead it to suspend hypermarket operations from November 27. Yonhap reported that court and creditor discussions and due diligence still follow the receipt of acquisition interest. Temporary closure is neither permanent shutdown nor a completed sale. Check the individual store and its tenants before visiting.

School non-regular workers are striking for one day on November 27 after wage talks failed. Replacement meals, packed lunches and alternative care arrangements vary by school. Do not assume every school's meals and care are suspended; check its notice first.

SBS reported that leaked KEPCO customer data was being traded on Telegram and that KEPCO confirmed the connection to the attack. There is no reason to seek out or buy stolen records. Use official channels rather than unsolicited links offering identity checks or compensation.

Culture: attendance, reservations and releases differ

Citing KOBIS data at midnight on November 27, Economy Review reported a 29.9% advance-booking share for Dune: Part Three, due December 16, and 8.7% for the film Pokseol. These are reservations for future screenings, not that day's admissions ranking.

ATEEZ's GOLDEN HOUR: Part.6 was released on November 13 according to its agency. Release information does not establish a Billboard chart position. No unverified chart number is added here.

Seoul weather: check rain as well as temperature

The 07:45 Seoul weather reading was 7.5°C, feeling like 5.7°C. Today's forecast high is 15.2°C, low 6.4°C, with a maximum daily precipitation probability of 76%. A probability for one hour is different from the day's maximum. Check the hourly forecast before leaving and consider an umbrella. Forecasts vary by provider and location.
