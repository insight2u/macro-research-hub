A 3.25% rate, a deferred fund review and a US market holiday

November 27 morning edition | Fifteen articles separate announcements, execution and results

KOENJAZHES

Interest rates rose on the day growth forecasts improved. Parliament deferred parts of the future-response fund review, and expanded public-housing recruitment followed a different calendar from move-in. US markets were closed for Thanksgiving on November 26. Today we ask which stage each large number describes. This briefing summarizes the linked articles and public evidence; it is not a read-aloud script.

Politics: a deferred review is not a policy verdict

Yonhap's November 26 afternoon report says decisions on several fund projects were deferred amid party disagreements about revenue sustainability, parliamentary control and future-industry investment. Each side's forecasts remain its claims. Beginning scrutiny is not final passage. This briefing does not verify later agreements or votes.

Economy and world: forecasts, decisions and trading dates

The Bank of Korea raised its rate from 3.00% to 3.25% and forecast growth of 3.9% in 2026 and 4.1% in 2027. Growth is a forecast; the rate is a decision. Preparing a stabilization fund is also different from buying shares. No new regular-session close exists for the US November 26 holiday. The November 27 stock early close is 3 AM November 28 in Korea. We do not invent current index, exchange-rate or VIX figures.

Society and industry: exposure is not causation

The finding that 94% of lost youth jobs were in high-AI-exposure industries does not estimate that AI directly caused 94% of the decline. The 56,000 capital-region public homes are a 2027 recruitment plan, not completed move-ins. Hyundai appointments assign tasks rather than prove results. Organoids and space welding are read as models and plans, not established treatment or industrial success.

Culture, daily life and history: preserve the boundary of review

The Kim Yun Shin guide uses official information rather than a claimed gallery visit. The dental book introduction is based on publisher material, not a full reading. Cross-border shopping needs delivery, compatibility and return checks beyond discounts; Daltanious needs distinct preorder and release dates. The history article follows the institution built between Nobel's November 27, 1895 signature and the first awards in 1901.

Different reference sessions

Korea's November 26 closes were KOSPI 4,711.21 (+0.29%) and KOSDAQ 655.71 (+0.78%). USD/KRW was 1,342.7 at the 15:30 reference, down 0.5 won. The latest row in Cboe's daily VIX source is 28.29 for November 25. These are dated snapshots, not live quotes; the Korean session and the US November 26 stock holiday are separate.
