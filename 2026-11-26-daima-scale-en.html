Small Goku, a world worth walking again

DAIMA as a form of adventure, not a 2026 premiere

KOENJAZH

Toei’s official synopsis describes a conspiracy that makes Goku and his companions small and sends them toward a new world. Japanese broadcast ended on February 28, 2025. This article does not present the show as newly airing in 2026.

Change the conditions, not only the power ranking

Our reading is that a smaller body can change distance, movement and the meaning of tools. A story need not expand only by adding a stronger enemy. We do not invent battle outcomes or discuss the ending.

What the official trailer promised

Toei’s July 2024 trailer describes new characters and an October 2024 release. That future-tense announcement belongs to its original date. A trailer explains a promise; it cannot establish the quality of the entire series.

Attribute the creator’s role precisely

Fuji TV credits Akira Toriyama with original work, story and character design. Those verified roles are not the same as saying one person made every scene. Animation remains a collaboration of many credited functions.

A point of entry

Learning a new world’s rules can offer an entry for both younger and long-time viewers. That is an editorial suggestion. Current Korean platform rights, prices and episode availability were not checked, so no promise of full-series access is made.
