Three financial safeguards lock three different doors

Distinguishing credit, remote account opening and open banking blocks

KOENJAZHES

If you are concerned about transactions in your name, does enabling one safety block stop everything? Korea's Financial Services Commission describes separate safeguards for credit transactions, remote account opening and open banking. Similar names do not mean identical coverage. To use protection effectively, understand which risks are reduced in return for less convenience.

Credit and bank accounts are different doors

The credit-transaction block prevents credit transactions in an individual's name, including unsecured loans, card loans and new credit-card issuance. The remote account-opening block aims to reduce the risk that an account is opened without the person's knowledge and used for crime. Neither is a complete substitute for the other.

A door for an account that already exists

The open-banking block stops the open-banking service to prevent unauthorized account-information access and transfers through that channel. It is not insurance guaranteeing reimbursement for every financial scam or a freeze on every transaction route. Impersonation and malicious apps outside the block's scope still require separate precautions.

Think about removal before enabling it

The commission's February 2026 guidance describes applying at an existing financial institution's branch or through Account Info and bank apps, among other channels. To prevent unauthorized removal, it says unblocking requires in-person identity verification at a branch. Check currently available channels with the financial institution. Start from a familiar official app or independently verified main number, not a link supplied by a caller. This article explains the schemes; it has not applied for anything or changed an account.
