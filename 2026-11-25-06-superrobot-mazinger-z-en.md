## From remote-controlled robots to a robot you ride

Mazinger Z aired in Japan from December 3, 1972 to September 1, 1974, for a total of 92 episodes. Created by Go Nagai and produced by Toei Animation. Unlike remote-controlled robots such as Tetsujin 28, the hero Koji Kabuto docks the Hover Pilder onto the robot's head and rides it himself. That invention is why the series is counted as the starting point of the super robot genre that later led to Gundam and Evangelion. The Korean YouTube channel 'Suspicious Ajae' (@Mr.X_creative) titled its Mazinger Z retrospective 'The beginning of super robots! Gundam and Evangelion started here too.'

The plot is simple, and that is the charm. Mazinger Z, loaded with super-scientific weapons, fights the mechanical beast army of Dr. Hell, who plots to conquer the earth. Breast Fire, Rocket Punch, Rust Hurricane. If you can still hear those attack calls just from the names, you are exactly our reader.

## 'Chogokin' was originally the name of Mazinger Z's armor

The word 'Chogokin', common in toy shops, also came from Mazinger Z. In the show, the robot's body is made of a fictional metal called 'Chogokin Z'. In February 1974 the toy company Popy (now part of the Bandai Spirits family) released a die-cast toy under that name, turning it into a toy brand. Bandai Spirits says on its official story page that the Chogokin series marked its 50th anniversary in 2024. That weighty feel of a block of steel in both hands - it is still somewhere in a middle-aged man's drawer.
