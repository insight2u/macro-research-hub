Today’s Book: rereading a day through music

Kim Jung-won’s new collection of essays

KOENJAZH

Pianist Kim Jung-won’s 나의 찬란한 그대에게 was published by Book Romance on November 25. KOBIC and bookseller records list 420 pages and ISBN 9791124038994. This is a new-book introduction based on the publisher’s description, contents and previews, not a review of the whole book.

A short author introduction

Kim works as a pianist and classical-radio host. The publisher presents writing that connects the stage, everyday life and memories with music. The useful question is what moves between performance, speech and writing, rather than accumulating biographical superlatives.

Do readers need musical training?

Public descriptions suggest essays about life and memory rather than a performance-technique manual. We suggest asking what a piece recalls in your own life, not how many composer names you know. This is a reading suggestion, not a guarantee about the entire book’s difficulty.

Why include a live performance?

The CBS Radio performance with Kim and Libelante offers a separate example of the author working through music. It is not identified as content from the book. A short note about your own memory after listening can help you compare the public preview with your interests.

Before buying

Check the physical size, length and sentence rhythm in the preview. Price, stock and delivery are not guaranteed. The YES24 detail page’s author, publisher and date were compared with the publishing record; the verified link is supplied below.
