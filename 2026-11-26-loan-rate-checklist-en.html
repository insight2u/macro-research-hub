You do not repay your loan at the average rate

New lending, outstanding balances and the cost of refinancing

KOENJAZH

Reports on the October bank-rate release put the new-household-loan average at 5.02% and the mortgage average at 4.98%, up 0.10 percentage points. These describe newly extended loans, not every borrower’s contract.

Know the denominator

The Bank of Korea’s official summary gives 4.64% for all new bank lending and 3.62% for new savings deposits. The 5.02% household figure comes from reporting on the detailed statistics. All lending, household lending and outstanding balances are different groups.

Policy rates and your rate can diverge

The accompanying historical YTN report explains market rates, bank bonds, COFIX, bank spreads and lending restrictions. Its headline about a 6% range concerns quoted rate bands. It is not today’s minimum offer and does not contradict a 4.98% monthly statistical average.

Simple arithmetic, detailed contracts

On an interest-only assumption, a one-percentage-point increase on 100 million won adds one million won in annual interest. This is an illustration. Actual monthly payments depend on amortization and remaining term. Include early-repayment charges, conditions for discounts and reset dates.

Four fields before refinancing

Write down the balance, actual rate, next reset date and total switching cost. An unapproved limit or advertised minimum is not a confirmed offer. This is general information, not an instruction to take a loan or buy a financial product.
