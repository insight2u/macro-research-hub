The regional AIDC exemption proposal is not yet in force

Separate the November 20 proposals from measures already enacted

Published 22 November 2026 · Policy announcement: 20 November

한국어English简体中文日本語

Read the verbs, not only the headline

On November 20, 2026, the government announced a draft decree setting a 50MW-or-less contracted-power threshold for assessment exemptions at qualifying non-capital-region AIDCs. The ICT ministry's release gives November 20–December 30 as the consultation period for the subordinate legislation. A proposal under consultation is not already a final rule applying to every project.[1][2]

Three changes, three different effects

First is an exemption from power-system impact assessment below a specified capacity outside the capital region. Second is planned simplification for AIDCs in special zones under the Act: non-technical assessment would be removed regardless of size, while technical assessment would remain. Third is priority assessment for megaprojects and AIDCs applying for integrated permitting under the Act. Exemption, partial simplification and queue priority are not identical benefits.[1]

The announcement does not erase existing rules

The climate ministry's November notice explains a differentiated passing score of 75 for capital-region data centres, a ban on transferring assessment result notices, and a standard one-year validity period. These adopted assessment rules must be separated from improvements being prepared for the AIDC Act's March 10, 2027 commencement. Project-specific application depends on final legislation and transitional provisions.[1][9]

Four stages matter to readers

Distinguish announcement, consultation, final enactment and commencement, and application to a particular project. The November 20 announcement sets a direction for reducing assessment burdens and waiting times; it does not fix any project's power-delivery date. Results should be tested against actual processing, connection and construction schedules. This is policy explanation, not legal advice on an individual project.
