A notice is not a key to the door

KO · EN · JA · ZH

About this cartoon

The calendar offers a key that remains out of reach. This criticizes counting application announcements as completed housing, not the value of broader access. The calendar, key and stamps are symbols, not evidence of delay or wrongdoing in a particular project.

Verified facts and source

in the capital region in 2027. Applications are not move-ins.
