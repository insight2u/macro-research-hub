Time spent looking at wood in Kim Yun Shin's In the Continuum

Questions for viewing, based on the gallery's public exhibition guide

KOENJAZHES

Kim Yun Shin's solo exhibition In the Continuum runs at Lehmann Maupin in Seoul from November 5 through December 24. We compared the News Factory arts discussion with the gallery's official material. This is a guide proposing questions for a visit, not a review claiming firsthand attendance.

Look for the body's time before naming the material

The gallery says the exhibition brings together sculpture, painting and what the artist calls painting-sculpture. Wood is not merely a background: it is touched, cut and left behind. The editors' first question is less what an object resembles than which actions shaped it. Distinguish surface and interior, remaining mass and empty space. The impression of the actual work is for visitors to discover themselves.

Addition and division are not only opposites

The official account explains Add Two Add One, Divide Two Divide One through adding, dividing and relations with nature. The title need not be memorized as a philosophical answer. It can begin questions about what was added or removed and how different surfaces connect within one mass. This is a viewing suggestion grounded in introductory material, not a judgment based on seeing every work.

Work continues after long journeys

The official biography records her birth in Wonsan in 1935, study in France, move to Argentina in 1984 and work across several places. Age and an unusual biography should not substitute for looking at art. Since past and present works are displayed together, visitors can compare continuity of language and changes of material. These sources alone do not justify claims that a particular work was the first or a final conclusion.

Check current visiting information

The official Seoul listing gives 213 Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu, Tuesday through Saturday, 11 AM–7 PM. The exhibition ends December 24. We have not verified admission fees, reservations or accessibility and therefore do not claim that admission is free or booking unnecessary. Check the official information before visiting.
